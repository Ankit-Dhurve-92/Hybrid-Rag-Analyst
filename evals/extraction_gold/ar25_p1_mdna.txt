Management Discussion and Analysis
Revenue
Revenue from operations grew 6.1% to ₹162,990 crore in FY2024-25, from ₹153,670 crore in FY2023-24.
Growth was supported by financial services and manufacturing, with industry revenue of ₹40,746 crore and ₹26,730 crore respectively.
Margins
Employee benefit expenses and operating discipline supported consolidated operating performance. Profit for the year was ₹26,713 crore.
Risks
Key risks include currency movement, slower discretionary technology spending, wage inflation, geopolitical uncertainty and data-security threats.
Outlook
For FY2025-26, Infosys expects continued growth led by large digital transformation and AI programs.
